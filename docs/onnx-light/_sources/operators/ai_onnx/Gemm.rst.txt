.. _op_ai_onnx_Gemm:

Gemm
====

- **Domain**: ``ai.onnx``
- **Since version**: 29

Generalized matrix multiplication: Y = alpha \* A' \* B' + beta \* C.
A has rank at least 1 and B has rank 2. transA swaps A's final two axes
and has no effect on vectors; transB transposes B. For A' of shape (..., K)
and B' of shape (K, N), Y has shape (..., N).
C is optional and must be unidirectionally broadcastable to Y.
If C is absent or beta is zero, its contribution and data read are skipped.

**Inputs**

- **A** (*T*): Rank >= 1 tensor. After applying transA, its shape is (..., K).
- **B** (*T*): Rank 2 tensor. Its shape is (K, N) if transB is 0, or (N, K) otherwise.
- **C** (*T*): Optional bias, unidirectionally broadcastable to output shape (..., N). If absent, the computation is done as if C is a scalar 0.

**Outputs**

- **Y** (*T*): Output tensor of shape (..., N).

**Attributes**

- **alpha** (*float*): Scales the matrix product.
- **beta** (*float*): Scales the bias.
- **transA** (*int*): Transposes the final two axes of A.
- **transB** (*int*): Transposes B.

**Type Constraints**

- **T**: Constrain input and output types to float/int tensors.
  Allowed types: tensor(bfloat16), tensor(double), tensor(float), tensor(float16), tensor(int32), tensor(int64), tensor(uint32), tensor(uint64).
