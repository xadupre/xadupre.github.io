PagedAttention
==============

- **Domain**: ``ai.rt``
- **Since version**: 1

Appends immutable KV pages and computes grouped-query attention for finite Q/K/V tensors of shape [batch,heads,L,D]. Q may have a multiple of the K/V head count; K and V have equal head counts and all inputs have equal batch and new sequence dimensions. The past/present values are structured caches whose blocks form a dynamic sequence of pages with INT64 scalar start/length and logical key/value tensors [batch,kv_heads,capacity,head_size]. Runtime pages may store dense FLOAT/FLOAT16/BFLOAT16 or inline affine integer data. Shape inference produces Y with Q's batch, head and sequence dimensions, V's head size and the input floating-point type. Storage policy belongs to the registered kernel and is not part of the operator attributes.

**Inputs**

- **Q** (*T*): Queries [batch,q_heads,L,key_head_size].
- **K** (*T*): New keys [batch,kv_heads,L,key_head_size].
- **V** (*T*): New values [batch,kv_heads,L,value_head_size].
- **past** (*C*): Paged cache structured value.

**Outputs**

- **Y** (*T*): Attention result [batch,q_heads,L,value_head_size].
- **present** (*C*): Cache after appending the new pages.

**Attributes**

- **block_size** (*int*): Positive maximum token capacity for each new page.
- **is_causal** (*int*): Whether to apply causal masking; only 0 or 1 is valid.
- **left_window_size** (*int*): Past-token window; -1 is unbounded and non-negative values limit it.
- **max_tokens** (*int*): Positive maximum retained token count, including past and new tokens.

**Type Constraints**

- **T**: Constrain Q/K/V and Y to one floating-point type.
  Allowed types: tensor(bfloat16), tensor(float), tensor(float16).
- **C**: Named paged-cache structure.
  Allowed types: struct.
